Who we are

A family atelier with a modern outlook

We believe jewellery should be personal, honest and made to be worn — not locked away. Every design starts as a sketch, is cast in our workshop and finished by hand.

Our founders, Imran and Sadia Qureshi, built Aurelia around transparent pricing, certified materials and relationships that last longer than a single purchase. Many of our clients now bring their children to choose their first pieces.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Our concierge team replies within one working day.

Ask a question {{icon:arrow-right size-4}}
Is your gold hallmarked?

Yes. Every gold piece is tested and hallmarked for purity, and your certificate lists the karat and weight.

Do diamonds come with a certificate?

All centre stones above 0.30ct ship with an independent grading report, and smaller stones with our own assurance card.

Can you create a custom design?

Absolutely. Bring an idea or a reference photo and our designers will prepare a 3D preview. Most commissions take 3–5 weeks.

What is your return policy?

Ready-to-ship pieces can be returned or exchanged within 30 days in original condition. Custom orders are non-refundable but covered by our lifetime care.
